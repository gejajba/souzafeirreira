## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the stately gravitas, intellectual rigor, and bespoke precision of high-end corporate advocacy. Built for discerning private and institutional clients seeking high-stakes legal counsel, the aesthetic eschews generic legal clichés—gavel motifs, loud stock photography, and aggressive call-to-actions—in favor of an architectural, editorial layout reminiscent of heritage print publications and luxury ateliers.

The design philosophy aligns with **Modern Editorial Luxury**:
- **Controlled Restraint:** Expansive negative space, razor-thin hairline dividers, and deliberate pacing project institutional confidence, permanence, and complete alignment with the ethical norms of legal advocacy.
- **Architectural Composition:** Asymmetric balance, oversized typographic indices, and generous vertical rhythm convey authority without friction.
- **Duality of Atmosphere:** Strategic tonal shifts alternate between luminous alabaster surfaces for analytical clarity and deep nocturnal navy blocks for institutional presence and confidential consultation.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture relies on nuanced contrasts that mirror parchment, deep ink, and refined metallic foil:

- **Primary (`#101D2B` - Deep Navy):** Used for primary typography on light surfaces, monolithic dark anchor sections, and structural headers. Conveys judicial gravitas and steadfast security.
- **Secondary (`#AF9270` - Muted Warm Gold):** Reserved strictly for structural cues, numerical indices (e.g., `01`, `02`), fine horizontal hairline rules, and restrained focus accents. Avoid loud saturation.
- **Tertiary (`#253B4A` - Slate Blue):** Bridges deep navy and mid-tones, utilized for contextual navigation bars, secondary containers, and refined interactive hover states.
- **Neutral (`#FAFAF8` - Soft Warm White):** The core canvas background, providing warmth and reducing the stark glare of clinical pure white.
- **Surface Subtle (`#F1EEE8` - Light Sand):** Deployed for alternating sections, editorial callout panels, and muted quotation fields to break reading fatigue.
- **Text Muted (`#667078` - Slate Grey):** Empowers secondary hierarchy, meta descriptions, and date stamps without competing against the deep navy prose.
- **Borders:** Ultra-fine architectural borders rendered via `border-light` on bright canvas and `border-dark` within deep navy passages.

## Typography

Typography establishes an immediate dialogue between classical humanism and contemporary clarity:

- **Primary Display & Headlines (Playfair Display):** Selected for its razor-sharp brackets, high vertical contrast, and authoritative editorial presence. Headlines favor standard sentence casing or curated title casing with relaxed, proportional spacing.
- **Body & Structural Text (Manrope):** Delivers clean geometry with open apertures and neutral cadence. Generous line heights (`1.6` to `1.7`) prevent cognitive fatigue across dense legal analyses, disclaimers, and contractual summaries.
- **Categorical & Overline Labels:** Rendered in small caps via `label-caps`, using deliberate tracking (`0.12em`) to ground navigational elements and category taxonomies.
- **Indices (`label-index`):** Applied to practice area sequencing (`01`, `02`, `03`) to introduce a refined serif inflection alongside technical data.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column asymmetric fluid grid with disciplined margins and generous breathing room:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max layout container capped at 1440px. Gutters set to `2rem`, outer canvas margins to `4rem`. Sections alternate between single-column editorial prose (columns 3–10) and off-center pairings (e.g., sticky narrative summary spanning 4 columns, detailed practice breakdown spanning 8 columns).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` canvas margins. Asymmetric pairings collapse smoothly into stacked modules separated by full-width 1px hairline dividers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` canvas margins. Fluid edge-to-edge section dividers ensure vertical rhythm without clutter.
- **Section Spacing:** Major theme transitions switch cleanly between soft warm white and deep navy utilizing `space-3xl` (6rem) vertical block padding for calm, monumental section pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects skeuomorphic drop shadows, heavy blur filters, and multi-layered float effects in favor of **Tonal Layering and Architectural Hairlines**:

- **Hairline Structural Planes:** Visual boundaries are articulated through razor-thin 1px borders (`rgba(16, 29, 43, 0.08)` on light surfaces; `rgba(250, 250, 248, 0.12)` on deep navy).
- **Tonal Stepping:** Elevation is communicated by changing surface tone rather than casting shadows. An active item moves from `#FAFAF8` to `#F1EEE8` or subtly introduces `#AF9270` borders.
- **Restrained Floating Overlays:** For necessary modal dialogues or global overlays (e.g., confidential contact drawers), use a disciplined, hyper-diffused ambient shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(16, 29, 43, 0.12)`, anchored by a 1px border.

## Shapes

The design language is strictly **Sharp (`0`)**. 

- **Architectural Edges:** Buttons, form inputs, table structures, and editorial panels utilize crisp 90-degree corners (`0px` radius).
- **Design Intent:** Rounded "pill" buttons and bubbly cards evoke consumer-grade software; sharp geometry evokes the permanence of chiselled stone, parchment briefs, and institutional order.
- **Internal Accents:** Hairline badges and subtle chip indicators also preserve razor-sharp rectilinear perimeters.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Navy (`#101D2B`) background, Soft Warm White (`#FAFAF8`) text, sharp corners, padded at `1rem 2rem`. Hover state triggers a subtle background shift to Slate Blue (`#253B4A`) with an eased 200ms transition.
- **Secondary (Outline):** 1px border in Primary Navy or Gold (`#AF9270`), transparent background, navy typography. Hover fills the surface with `accent-gold-soft` or solid gold with inverted white text.
- **Editorial Text Link:** Understated typography with a persistent 1px hairline bottom rule spaced 4px below the baseline. Hover transforms the rule color from navy to gold.

### Practice Area Lists (Replacing Cards)
- Practice areas avoid generic "cards" and are instead structured as **Numbered Horizontal Accordion Rows**:
  - Each item features a top hairline border, an index number in gold (`01`, `02`, `03` in `Playfair Display`), the practice title, an excerpt, and an understated directional arrow icon.
  - Hover triggers an effortless background tint to `#F1EEE8` (or `#253B4A` on dark) and horizontal translation of the arrow by 4px.

### Form Inputs & Textareas
- Minimalist architectural field design: 1px bottom border only (`border-light`) or a clean 4-sided sharp border with transparent fill.
- Placeholder text in `text-muted` (`#667078`).
- Focus state eliminates glowing browser halos; it transitions the bottom border cleanly to `#AF9270` with a 1px solid stroke.

### Editorial Quotes & Disclaimers
- Prominent partner statements and ethical legal notes are styled with a left 1px gold hairline divider (`#AF9270`) and padded left by `space-lg` (1.5rem), set in `editorial-quote`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Precise square geometry (0px radius) framed by a 1px border in `#101D2B`.
- Checked state fills with `#101D2B` featuring a crisp geometric white mark, matching the firm's strict structural aesthetic.
